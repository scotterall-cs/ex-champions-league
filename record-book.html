Things worth knowing

The most violent game ever played was Joe hanging 205.84 on Sam in week 5 of 2019 — a 116.20-point win that remains both the highest single score and the largest margin in league history.

Dan founded this thing, not Tyler. Yahoo lists Dan as commissioner in 2014 and 2015 with Tyler as his co-commissioner. Tyler has run it since 2017.

Dan’s team was called “BXK Sux” for years — bxkeegan sucks. He named his franchise after trash-talking Brian, and kept it that way through at least 2021.

Greg has the longest win streak of the Sleeper era, at nine. The easiest schedule ever played (88.99 a week, 2017) belongs to Drew Jacoby — whose franchise Greg took over in 2019.

Brian owns the floor. The three lowest scores of the Sleeper era are all his, including a 27.50 in week 12 of 2025 — from a roster that could have started 61.80.

Detrick has two rings and the worst losing streak on record. Champion in 2014 and 2020, then nine straight defeats. Sam owns the other end — 13 losses in 2014 is still the most in a single season.

Scott manages better than anyone. The highest lineup efficiency in the league, the fewest points left on the bench, and the 2021 title.
