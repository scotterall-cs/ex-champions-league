What the record supports

The championship count is dead level at six apiece across twelve seasons, and it has never been more than one apart. ’11 leads on playoff places and head-to-head games, but it fields seven teams to ’12’s five.

2025 was the most lopsided season on record. ’11 took five of the six playoff places and won the cross-class schedule 28–16. It is the only reason ’11 leads the game count; strip it out and ’12 is up 26–21.

Five playoff places belong to managers who have since left — Drew Jacoby, Ned Lundvall and Skylar W. Greg took over Drew’s franchise in 2019; the current twelve were all in place by 2020.

Dan and Tyler have three titles each, one from each class, and Detrick has two.
